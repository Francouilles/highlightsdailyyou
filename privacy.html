Privacy Policy

highlightsdailyyou is a personal tool used by one creator for their own TikTok account. This page explains what data it handles.

  • Data handled. After the operator authorises the tool through TikTok Login Kit, it holds an access token and a refresh token for the operator's own account, and the videos and captions the operator chooses to publish.
  • Storage. Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API endpoints.
  • Other users. The tool does not collect, read, store, sell or share personal data about any other TikTok user, and has no visitors' accounts, cookies or analytics.
  • Revocation. The operator can revoke the tool's access at any time in the TikTok app settings (Security and permissions, Manage app permissions).
  • Contact. Questions can be sent through the TikTok account @highlightsdailyyou.
